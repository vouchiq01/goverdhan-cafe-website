Privacy Policy

This website is an information page for Govardhan Cafe. We respect your privacy and collect as little as possible.

What we collect

This site has no accounts, logins or payments, and it does not store any personal data on a server. The WhatsApp buttons only open a pre-written message on your own device, and you choose whether to send it.

WhatsApp messages

If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name, phone number and the details you share. We use them only to reply to you and handle your order or enquiry. We never sell or share them for marketing. WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies to those messages.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps (embedded map and directions)
  • Google Fonts (text styling)
  • Unsplash (some food photos)
  • Swiggy (online ordering, on their own site)

These services may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies ourselves. The embedded Google Map may set its own cookies.
